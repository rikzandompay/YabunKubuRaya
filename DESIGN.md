# DESIGN SPECIFICATION (DESIGN.md)
## Website Resmi Yayasan Bakti Umat Nusantara Cabang Pontianak

### 1. Prinsip Desain & Tema Visual
* **Tema Utama:** Modern, Islami Kontemporer, Bersih, dan Terpercaya (*Trustworthy*).
* **Nuansa:** Memberikan kesan hangat, profesional, akuntabel untuk lembaga sosial/yayasan.
* **Pendekatan:** *Mobile-First*, Responsif, Aksesibel.

---

### 2. Sistem Warna (Color Palette)

| Kategori | Warna | Hex Code | Pengunaan Utama |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Deep Emerald Green | `#065F46` | Header, Utama Brand, Button Primary, Accent |
| **Secondary** | Warm Amber Gold | `#D97706` | Highlight Donasi, Badge, CTA Accent |
| **Dark Neutral** | Slate Grey | `#1E293B` | Teks Utama, Heading, Footer Dark |
| **Light Neutral** | Off-White / Cream | `#F8FAFC` | Background Halaman & Card |
| **Surface White** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Form Input, Card Content, Modal |

---

### 3. Tipografi (Typography)

* **Font Utama (Body & UI):** `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `sans-serif`
* **Heading (Judul Utama):** `Poppins` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (SemiBold - Bold)

| Elemen | Ukuran Screen | Weight | Line Height |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Title (H1)** | 32px (Mobile) / 48px (Desktop) | Bold (700) | 1.2 |
| **Section Title (H2)**| 24px (Mobile) / 32px (Desktop) | SemiBold (600) | 1.3 |
| **Subheading (H3)** | 18px (Mobile) / 20px (Desktop) | Medium (500) | 1.4 |
| **Body Text** | 14px (Mobile) / 16px (Desktop) | Regular (400) | 1.6 |
| **Caption & Meta** | 12px / 13px | Regular (400) | 1.5 |

---

### 4. Komponen UI Standar

#### A. Tombol (Buttons)
* **Primary CTA (Donasi):** Background Emerald `#065F46`, Teks Putih, Rounded 8px, Hover: `#047857` + Soft Shadow.
* **Secondary CTA (Kontak/Informasi):** Border 1.5px `#065F46`, Teks `#065F46`, Background Transparan, Hover: `#F0FDF4`.

#### B. Kartu (Cards & Containers)
* **Program & Berita Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, Border 1px `#E2E8F0`, Radius 12px, Shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`, Hover effect: lift up 4px + border highlight.

#### C. Input Form & Modal (Admin Panel & Form Kontak)
* **Input Field:** Border 1px `#CBD5E1`, Radius 6px, Focus State: Ring 2px `#065F46`.

---

### 5. Tata Letak (Layout & Grid System)
* **Container Max Width:** 1280px (Desktop XL), 1024px (Desktop), 100% dengan Margin 16px (Mobile).
* **Grid Layout:** 12-Column Grid (Desktop), 1-2 Column (Mobile).
* **Spacing Scale:** Baseline 8px (8px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px).
